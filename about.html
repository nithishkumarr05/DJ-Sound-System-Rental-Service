About Us

Born In A Garage.
Raised On The Dancefloor.

BeatBox started in a cramped LA garage with two speakers, one mixer, and a promise: to bring club-quality sound to every party we touched. A decade later, that same promise still drives everything we do.

We've grown into a full-service sound and lighting company — but we still obsess over every cable, every EQ curve, and every last detail that makes your event feel effortless to your guests.

Our Experience

A Decade Of Loud Moments

From backyard parties to festival stages — a look at the milestones that shaped who we are.

2016 The Beginning

Two Speakers In A Garage

Two friends, one van, and the first paid gig for a friend's birthday. Word spread fast.

500-Person Outdoor Stage

Our first Funktion-One rig, our first full lighting install, and the night BeatBox became a "real company."

2022 Expansion

2026 Today

The biggest year yet — and we're still tuning every rig by ear, the same way we did in 2016.
