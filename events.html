Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common logistical questions about venue permits, power, load-in, and equipment backup.

How far in advance should we reserve equipment for our event?
We recommend reserving 3 to 6 months in advance for peak wedding season (May through October) and corporate holiday galas (November/December). For private parties and club nights, we can often accommodate bookings within 1 to 2 weeks subject to equipment availability.
Are setup, soundcheck, and strike times billed into our rental hours?
No, never! Our technician arrival, physical load-in, cable masking, and room acoustic soundcheck are 100% complimentary and completed well before your guests enter. Your contracted technician hours only start when your event officially begins.
Can our guest DJ, student performer, or live band plug into the rig?
Yes! All BeatBox systems feature universal XLR, 1/4", and digital inputs. Guest DJs can easily dock their laptop or USB drives, and live acoustic acts can patch into our mixing console. Our on-site sound engineer manages the levels to keep transitions seamless.
What power requirements are needed, and do you supply generators?
Standard packages operate efficiently on standard 120V / 15-20A wall outlets. For outdoor festivals, beachfront ceremonies, or remote parks without grid power, we supply whisper-quiet inverter power generators with surge suppression as an affordable add-on.
Are you insured for university campuses, public parks, and luxury venues?
Yes. BeatBox carries comprehensive $2,000,000 commercial general liability insurance. We regularly provide Certificates of Insurance (COI) naming your venue, university, or city park as additionally insured at zero extra charge.
