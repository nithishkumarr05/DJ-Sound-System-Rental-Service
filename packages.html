Package FAQs

Everything you need to know about delivery, setup, payments, and equipment guarantees.

How do I know which package fits my venue and guest count?
As a rule of thumb: Basic ($199) handles up to 50 guests in home/backyard settings; Standard ($499) easily powers 50 to 250 guests in banquet halls, clubs, or indoor spaces; Wedding ($899) provides dual-zone audio for outdoor vows + indoor receptions up to 400 guests; and Premium ($1,299) is designed for 500 to 1,500+ attendees in concert amphitheaters or large outdoor festivals.
Are setup, soundcheck, and strike times deducted from rental hours?
No, never! Our technician arrival, load-in, room acoustic calibration, and soundcheck are completed before your event start time at zero extra charge. The 8 to 12 hours of included technician coverage begins when your event doors open.
Can I customize or swap items inside a standard package?
Absolutely. All of our packages serve as modular starting points. You can upgrade to Pioneer CDJ-3000s, add wireless uplighting packs, substitute microphones, or include cold spark machines. Simply mention your preferences when submitting your quote.
What happens if a piece of equipment fails during my event?
Every package above Basic includes on-site redundant backup gear (spare microphones, audio cables, and mixer backup). For our Standard, Wedding, and Premium packages, our on-site certified technician immediately resolves any technical glitch in seconds without interrupting the party.
What are your delivery areas and travel fees?
Delivery, load-in, and post-event teardown are 100% free across Los Angeles County. For events in Orange County, Ventura, San Diego, or Palm Springs, a small transparent mileage surcharge applies based on distance from our downtown LA warehouse.
How do I lock in my date, and what deposit is required?
We require a 25% refundable deposit to reserve the gear and crew for your date. The remaining balance is due 48 hours prior to delivery. Changes or cancellations up to 14 days before your event receive a 100% full refund.
